# Private Course Use Policy

## Project status

This is an internal course project for BITS Hyderabad, CS F407 Artificial Intelligence. The team does not plan to publish, publicly demonstrate, publicly host, or externally distribute the project or dataset material.

## Access (current team decisions)

- **GitHub repository:** public for now (decision 11). Because the repository is public, it may contain only code, documentation, and metadata. Videos, frames, stress images, DMD annotation files, event CSVs, and unsanitized RLDD metadata must never be committed.
- **Shared Google Drive folder (`processed_rldd`):** currently viewable by anyone with the link. The team accepted this for now (decision 12). Do not post the link anywhere public, and do not add it to the repository.
- **Local copies:** DMD videos, event files, and stress images are kept under `C:\NeuroNova_data` on Aadi's PC until they are copied to the team Drive.
- Do not share Kaggle tokens or other credentials in documents, chats, or code.

## Course submission

- Cite UTA-RLDD and DMD in the course report or presentation.
- Use aggregate metrics, tables, and diagrams instead of raw participant footage.
- UTA-RLDD: only 36 of the 60 participants allowed their faces to be published, and identities must not be revealed. Do not show RLDD faces in the report.
- DMD: do not include frames or stress images in the report unless the supervisor confirms this is allowed under the dataset terms.
- Do not upload datasets or derived images to a public repository, website, model hub, or file-sharing link.

## Dataset terms

Private course use does not remove provider requirements.

- **UTA-RLDD:** the provider page states no licence or redistribution terms. Any work reporting results must cite Ghoddoosian, Galib, and Athitsos, "A Realistic Dataset and Baseline Temporal Model for Early Drowsiness Detection", CVPR Workshops 2019 (arXiv:1904.07312).
- **DMD:** academic use only, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, users 18 or older, attribution required, no commercial use, and no distribution of modified material. Cite Ortega et al., "DMD: A Large-Scale Multi-modal Driver Monitoring Dataset for Attention and Alertness Analysis", ECCV Workshops 2020. The team uses the third-party Kaggle copy `momarberliansyah/dmd-video-v1` (decision 3). Name it as the source.
- Stress images derived from DMD are for restricted internal evaluation only. They must not be distributed.

## New human data

- No volunteers are recruited and no new recordings are made.
- No consent, withdrawal, or raw-video deletion procedure is needed, because no new data is collected.
- The supervisor may ask for a short ethics declaration stating that only existing datasets are used.
